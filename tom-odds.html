Tom Bin odds: the chance to succeed

Tom Bin, Man of Bree, Treasure Hunter, age 21. What he can do, how good he is, and the chance to succeed.

The short version

Best at (Strong)

  • Stealth and Scan: 90%
  • Then Courtesy, Persuade, Riddle: 50–54%

Fair with Hope

  • Insight, Craft, Enhearten
  • Spend 1 Hope and they go from Weak to about 50–70%

Bad at (Weak)

  • Awe, Healing, Battle, Lore: 8%
  • Fighting: Spear 13–27%, Bow 8–10%, with no help

Save Hope for the rolls that matter. Use Inspired with Burglary on sneaky rolls, and with Inquisitive on Insight, Riddle and Scan.

How to read a roll

  • Target Number (TN) = 20 minus the attribute. Strength 4 gives TN 16. Heart 4 gives TN 16. Wits 6 gives TN 13, because Prowess takes 1 off p.18 p.80.
  • The roll: one Feat die, plus one Success die for each skill rank. Add them up and reach the TN p.17.
  • ★ Favoured: roll 2 Feat dice and keep the better one p.20.
  • Hope: spend 1 Hope to add 1 die p.20.
  • Inspired: if one of Tom's Distinctive Features fits (Rustic, Inquisitive, Burglary), 1 Hope adds 2 dice p.20.
  • Useful item: outside a fight, a fitting item adds 1 die p.49. Wind-proof lantern for Scan. Homemade maps for Explore.
  • Weary (Endurance 17 or less): Success dice showing 1–3 count as 0. Every chance below drops p.23.
  • Special results: the Gandalf rune on the Feat die is an automatic success. The Eye counts as 0. A 6 on a Success die is an icon. A great success is a success with at least one 6.

Fighting

To hit, Tom's attack TN is his Strength TN of 16 plus the enemy's Parry p.65 p.98. A tougher enemy means a harder roll.
