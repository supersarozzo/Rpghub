Combos: "if this, then do this"

For your half-orc pirate Artillerist, level 3. Eight situations, one block each. Works offline. Prints on paper.

Your numbers: HP 24 · AC 15 (14 without Enhanced Defense) · spell DC 13 · spell attack +5 · speed 30 ft · Con save +4 · only 3 spell slots (level 1). PHB = Player's Handbook. T = Tasha's. Page refs are printed pages. Rules are in my own words.

1. Start of a fight

When: A fight is about to begin, and you have a turn or two to get ready.

  1. Action: build your cannon. Hold woodcarver's tools. Pick flat ground within 5 ft. It has AC 18 and 15 HP. T p.17-18
  2. Bonus action: activate it (you must be within 60 ft). Mourning Bell has no legs: it fires from your shoulder (one hand), or from where you set it down. Pick its type when you build it: Ballista, Flamethrower or Protector. T p.17-18
  3. Reaction: keep it for Shield. AC 15 becomes 20. PHB p.275
  4. Movement: step behind cover. PHB p.196
free cannon (1st of the day)1 slot for a 2nd cannonmove 30 ft

Example: Turn 1: you build Mourning Bell (action) and it fires at once (bonus action). You step behind a crate. Turn 2: crossbow +5, 1d8+3 (action). An enemy swings at you, so you cast Shield (reaction, 1 slot).

2. Many enemies

When: 3 or more enemies stand in a line or a clump, or crowd around you.

  1. Action (once): build the cannon as a Flamethrower. T p.18
  2. Bonus action (every turn): activate it. It sprays a 15-ft cone. Each enemy makes a DC 13 Dex save and takes 2d8 fire (half if it saves). Check that no friend stands in the cone. T p.18
  3. Action (if they crowd you): cast Thunderwave. It is a 15-ft cube. DC 13 Con save, 2d8 thunder, and failures are pushed 10 ft. It also pushes friends, and it is loud. PHB p.282
  4. Movement: walk away. Pushed enemies spend movement to catch up.
free cannon1 slot (Thunderwave)bonus action each turn

Example: Turn 1: the Flamethrower appears (action) and sprays (bonus action). Turn 2: Thunderwave (action, 1 slot) pushes the clump back. Mourning Bell sprays them again (bonus action). Turn 3: crossbow +5 at the weakest enemy (action).

3. One big enemy

When: One strong enemy is alone and tough. It may pound your friends.

  1. Action (once): build the cannon as a Force Ballista. T p.18
  2. Bonus action (every turn): activate it. Spell attack +5, 2d8 force, push 5 ft. The target can be up to 120 ft from the cannon. T p.18
  3. Action (every turn): crossbow with Repeating Shot: +5, 1d8+3, no loading. T p.22
  4. Action (instead, 1 slot): cast Faerie Fire so your whole team hits it with advantage. It can save (DC 13 Dex). PHB p.239
free cannonfree crossbow1 slot (Faerie Fire)concentration (Faerie Fire)

Example: Turn 1: the Ballista appears (action) and fires at once (bonus action). Turn 2: crossbow (action) +5, 1d8+3. Mourning Bell fires (bonus action) for 2d8 and pushes it 5 ft. If both hit, that is about 16 damage in a round.

4. Friend down (0 HP)

When: A friend is at 0 HP, or several friends are hurt.

  1. Movement: walk next to the friend, if needed.
  2. Action: cast Cure Wounds by touch. It heals 1d8+3 and wakes them up. PHB p.230 Out of slots? A Medicine check (DC 10) only stops the dying, with no HP back, but your bonus is +0, so about a 55% chance. PHB p.197
  3. Bonus action: if the cannon is a Protector, it gives 1d8+3 temp HP to friends within 10 ft. Temp HP do not wake a friend at 0 HP. T p.18
  4. Reaction: keep it for Shield.
1 slot (Cure Wounds)free cannonmove 30 ft

Example: A friend drops to 0 HP. You walk to them (movement) and cast Cure Wounds (action, 1 slot). They get up. You have only one cannon at a time. To change its type, dismiss it (action), then build a new one (action, and 1 slot if it is your 2nd today). T p.17

5. Enemy in my face

When: An enemy stands next to you, or an attack roll would hit you.

  1. Reaction: cast Shield when an attack would hit. +5 AC until your next turn, so AC 15 becomes 20. PHB p.275
  2. Action: Shocking Grasp: +5, 1d8 lightning. It has advantage against a metal-armored target. The enemy cannot take reactions until its next turn. PHB p.275
  3. Movement: walk away. If Shocking Grasp hit, the enemy has no reaction, so it gets no free hit as you leave. PHB p.195
  4. Bonus action: activate the cannon (stay within 60 ft of it).
1 slot (Shield)free cantripmove 30 ftcannon

Example: A pirate swings at you. Shield (reaction, 1 slot) makes AC 20, and it misses. Your turn: Shocking Grasp (action) for 1d8. You walk back (movement). Mourning Bell fires (bonus action). Your spear is weaker: +3, 1d6+1.

6. Can't see or hit it

When: The enemy is invisible, hiding, or hard to hit (high AC).

  1. Action: cast Faerie Fire on the area. DC 13 Dex save. Outlined creatures glow, cannot hide, and attacks against them have advantage. PHB p.239
  2. Bonus action: the cannon attacks too, with advantage.
  3. Action (no slot): Shocking Grasp if the enemy wears metal. Or force a save with the Flamethrower (DC 13 Dex) or Thunderwave (DC 13 Con). No attack roll needed. T p.18 PHB p.282
  4. Magical darkness or fog? Your darkvision may not help. Back into light and ask the DM what you can sense. PHB p.183
1 slot (Faerie Fire)concentration, 1 minutefree cantrip

Example: You hear a ghost-pirate you cannot see. Faerie Fire on its area (action, 1 slot, concentration). It glows. Your friends and Mourning Bell attack with advantage. If it hits you, roll Con +4 against DC 10, or half the damage if that is higher. PHB p.203

7. Out of slots

When: You have no spell slots left, or the cannon is gone.

  1. Action: crossbow with Repeating Shot: +5, 1d8+3, no loading. T p.22 Or Shocking Grasp if an enemy is close.
  2. Bonus action: activate the cannon. This costs no slot if it is already built.
  3. Action (other choices): Help (a friend gets advantage), Dodge, Disengage or Dash. PHB p.192
  4. Movement: get behind cover. Cover gives +2 AC. PHB p.196
  5. Shield is not possible now. Plan a long rest: short rests do not give slots back. PHB p.186
free crossbowfree cantripfree cannonmove 30 ft

Example: Your slots are gone. You step behind a barrel (movement), shoot the crossbow (action), and Mourning Bell fires (bonus action). Next turn a friend flanks an enemy, so you use Help (action) to give them advantage.

8. Exploring and talking

When: No fight now. You search, read a map, sense magic, or talk.

  1. Action: cast Guidance on whoever rolls. They add a d4 to one ability check. It does not help attacks or saves. PHB p.248
  2. Ritual (10 extra minutes): cast Detect Magic with no slot. You sense magic within 30 ft while you concentrate. Then roll Arcana +5. PHB p.231 T p.12
  3. Your skills: Investigation +5 to search, Intimidation +1 to scare. Cartographer's tools help with maps. PHB p.154
  4. Missing a tool? "The Right Tool for the Job" makes one set of artisan's tools in 1 hour. It cannot make thieves' tools. The set vanishes when you use the feature again. T p.13
no slotconcentration (Guidance, Detect Magic)free cantriptime: 10 min or 1 hour

Example: A locked crypt door. You cast Detect Magic as a ritual (10 minutes, no slot) and sense a faint glow in the lock. Investigation +5 finds the trap. A friend with thieves' tools gets Guidance. The door opens. Do not start this just before a fight.

The swappable bits (Enhanced Defense, Intimidation) are in CONFIG at the end of this file. Check numbers against your sheet. If the DM changes a rule, the DM wins.
