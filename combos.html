Combos: "if this, then do this"

For your half-orc pirate Artillerist, level 5. Twelve situations, one block each. Works offline. Prints on paper.

Your numbers: HP 38 · AC 17 (16 without Enhanced Defense) · spell DC 15 · spell attack +7 · speed 30 ft · Con save +5 · 6 spell slots (4 of level 1, 2 of level 2) · Misty Step free once per long rest. PHB = Player's Handbook. T = Tasha's. Page refs are printed pages. Rules are in my own words.

1. Start of a fight

When: A fight is about to begin, and you have a turn or two to get ready.

  1. Action: build your cannon. Hold woodcarver's tools. Pick flat ground within 5 ft. It has AC 18 and 25 HP. T p.17-18
  2. Bonus action: activate it (you must be within 60 ft). Mourning Bell has no legs: it fires from your shoulder (one hand), or from where you set it down. Pick its type when you build it: Ballista, Flamethrower or Protector. T p.17-18
  3. Reaction: keep it for Shield. AC 17 becomes 22. PHB p.275
  4. Movement: step behind cover. PHB p.196
free cannon (1st of the day)1 slot for a 2nd cannonmove 30 ft

Example: Turn 1: you build Mourning Bell (action) and it fires at once (bonus action). You step behind a crate. Turn 2: Fire Bolt +7, 2d10 (+1d8 with the rod) (action). An enemy swings at you, so you cast Shield (reaction, 1 slot).

2. Many enemies

When: 3 or more enemies stand in a line or a clump, or crowd around you.

  1. Action (once): build the cannon as a Flamethrower. T p.18
  2. Bonus action (every turn): activate it. It sprays a 15-ft cone. Each enemy makes a DC 15 Dex save and takes 2d8 fire (half if it saves). Check that no friend stands in the cone. T p.18
  3. Action (if they crowd you): cast Thunderwave. It is a 15-ft cube. DC 15 Con save, 2d8 thunder (+1d8 with the rod), and failures are pushed 10 ft. It also pushes friends, and it is loud. PHB p.282
  4. Action (clumped further away): cast Shatter (level-2 slot). A 10-ft sphere within 60 ft, DC 15 Con save, 3d8 thunder. PHB p.275
  5. Movement: walk away. Pushed enemies spend movement to catch up.
free cannon1 slot (Thunderwave or Shatter)bonus action each turn

Example: Turn 1: the Flamethrower appears (action) and sprays (bonus action). Turn 2: Thunderwave (action, 1 slot) pushes the clump back. Mourning Bell sprays them again (bonus action). Turn 3: Fire Bolt +7 at the weakest enemy (action).

3. One big enemy

When: One strong enemy is alone and tough. It may pound your friends.

  1. Action (once): build the cannon as a Force Ballista. T p.18
  2. Bonus action (every turn): activate it. Spell attack +7, 2d8 force, push 5 ft. The target can be up to 120 ft from the cannon. T p.18
  3. Action (every turn): Fire Bolt through the rod: +7, 2d10 + 1d8 fire. PHB p.242 T p.18
  4. Action (instead, a slot): cast Faerie Fire so your whole team hits it with advantage (DC 15 Dex), or Scorching Ray (level-2 slot, 3 rays of 2d6), or Heat Metal if it wears metal. PHB p.239 PHB p.273 PHB p.250
free cannonfree cantrip1 slot (Faerie Fire)concentration (Faerie Fire or Heat Metal)

Example: Turn 1: the Ballista appears (action) and fires at once (bonus action). Turn 2: Fire Bolt (action) +7, 2d10 + 1d8. Mourning Bell fires (bonus action) for 2d8 and pushes it 5 ft. If both hit, that is about 24 damage in a round.

4. Friend down (0 HP)

When: A friend is at 0 HP, or several friends are hurt.

  1. Movement: walk next to the friend, if needed.
  2. Action: cast Cure Wounds by touch. It heals 1d8+4 and wakes them up. Too far? The homunculus can deliver it (its reaction, up to 120 ft from you). PHB p.230 T p.22 Out of slots? The healer's kit stabilizes at once (no HP back). PHB p.151
  3. Bonus action: if the cannon is a Protector, it gives 1d8+4 temp HP to friends within 10 ft. Temp HP do not wake a friend at 0 HP. T p.18
  4. Reaction: keep it for Shield.
1 slot (Cure Wounds)free cannonmove 30 ft

Example: A friend drops to 0 HP. You walk to them (movement) and cast Cure Wounds (action, 1 slot). They get up. You have only one cannon at a time. To change its type, dismiss it (action), then build a new one (action, and 1 slot if it is your 2nd today). T p.17

5. Enemy in my face

When: An enemy stands next to you, or an attack roll would hit you.

  1. Reaction: cast Shield when an attack would hit. +5 AC until your next turn, so AC 17 becomes 22. PHB p.275
  2. Action: Shocking Grasp: +7, 2d8 lightning. It has advantage against a metal-armored target. The enemy cannot take reactions until its next turn. PHB p.275
  3. Movement: walk away. If Shocking Grasp hit, the enemy has no reaction, so it gets no free hit as you leave. PHB p.195
  4. Bonus action: activate the cannon (stay within 60 ft of it). Or Misty Step away (free once per long rest).
1 slot (Shield)free cantripmove 30 ftcannon or Misty Step

Example: A pirate swings at you. Shield (reaction, 1 slot) makes AC 22, and it misses. Your turn: Shocking Grasp (action) for 2d8. You walk back (movement). Mourning Bell fires (bonus action). Your dagger is weaker: +5, 1d4+2.

6. Can't see or hit it

When: The enemy is invisible, hiding, or hard to hit (high AC).

  1. Action: cast Faerie Fire on the area. DC 15 Dex save. Outlined creatures glow, cannot hide, and attacks against them have advantage. PHB p.239
  2. Bonus action: the cannon attacks too, with advantage.
  3. Action (no slot): Shocking Grasp if the enemy wears metal. Or force a save with the Flamethrower (DC 15 Dex), Thunderwave or Shatter (DC 15 Con). No attack roll needed. T p.18 PHB p.282 PHB p.275
  4. Magical darkness or fog? Your darkvision may not help. Back into light and ask the DM what you can sense. PHB p.183
1 slot (Faerie Fire)concentration, 1 minutefree cantrip

Example: You hear a ghost-pirate you cannot see. Faerie Fire on its area (action, 1 slot, concentration). It glows. Your friends and Mourning Bell attack with advantage. If it hits you, roll Con +5 against DC 10, or half the damage if that is higher. PHB p.203

7. Out of slots

When: You have no spell slots left, or the cannon is gone.

  1. Action: Fire Bolt: +7, 2d10 (+1d8 with the rod). PHB p.242 Or Shocking Grasp if an enemy is close.
  2. Bonus action: activate the cannon. This costs no slot if it is already built.
  3. Action (other choices): Help (a friend gets advantage), Dodge, Disengage or Dash. PHB p.192
  4. Movement: get behind cover. Cover gives +2 AC. PHB p.196
  5. Shield is not possible now. Plan a long rest: short rests do not give slots back. PHB p.186
free cantripfree cannonmove 30 ft

Example: Your slots are gone. You step behind a barrel (movement), cast Fire Bolt (action), and Mourning Bell fires (bonus action). Next turn a friend flanks an enemy, so you use Help (action) to give them advantage.

8. Exploring and talking

When: No fight now. You search, read a map, sense magic, or talk.

  1. Bonus action: send the homunculus ahead: it flies 30 ft, sees in the dark (60 ft), Perception +6, Stealth +5. Order it with your bonus action. T p.21-22
  2. Ritual (10 extra minutes): cast Detect Magic with no slot. You sense magic within 30 ft while you concentrate. Then roll Arcana +7. PHB p.231 T p.12
  3. Your skills: Investigation +7 to search, Intimidation +2 to scare. Cartographer's tools help with maps. PHB p.154
  4. Missing a tool? "The Right Tool for the Job" makes one set of artisan's tools in 1 hour. It cannot make thieves' tools. The set vanishes when you use the feature again. T p.13
no slotconcentration (Detect Magic)homunculustime: 10 min or 1 hour

Example: A locked crypt door. You cast Detect Magic as a ritual (10 minutes, no slot) and sense a faint glow in the lock. Investigation +7 finds the trap. The homunculus flies down the corridor first and sees nobody waiting. The door opens. Do not start this just before a fight.

9. Fire, cold or lightning hits me

When: A breath, a spell or a trap is about to deal acid, cold, fire, lightning or thunder damage to you.

  1. Reaction: cast Absorb Elements (1 slot). You resist that damage type until the start of your next turn, so this hit is halved. It is from Xanathar's Guide, which is not in your books: ask your DM.
  2. Not Shield: you have one reaction, and Shield does not help against a save-for-half breath. Shield is for attack rolls. Pick the right one.
  3. Your next turn: the first melee hit you land deals +1d6 of the same damage type. Use Shocking Grasp (+7) or the Moon-touched dagger (+5, 1d4+2). Fire Bolt is not a melee attack, so it gets no bonus.
  4. Bonus action: the cannon fires as normal.
1 slot (Absorb Elements)uses your reactionfree cantrip

Example: A young dragon breathes fire on the party. You fail the save and would take 28. You cast Absorb Elements (reaction) and take only 14. On your turn you step next to it and Shocking Grasp it (action): the hit adds 1d6 fire. The cannon fires too (bonus action).

10. Metal armor or a metal weapon

When: An enemy wears metal armor or holds a metal weapon (a knight, a guard, a pirate with a cutlass), and its armor is too hard to hit.

  1. Action: cast Heat Metal (level-2 slot, concentration). Pick a metal object you can see within 60 ft. It glows red-hot. Whoever holds or wears it takes 2d8 fire at once. There is no attack roll. PHB p.250
  2. Their turn: if they hold it, they make a Con save (DC 15) or drop it. A creature that keeps it, or wears it, has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of your next turn.
  3. Next turns (bonus action): burn them again for 2d8 fire, again with no roll. This is your bonus action, so the cannon does not fire that turn. Choose the better one: 2d8 sure fire damage, or the cannon's attack roll.
  4. Action: Shocking Grasp has advantage against metal armor: +7, 2d8 lightning. It also stops the enemy's reactions. PHB p.275
1 level-2 slot (Heat Metal)concentration, 1 minutebonus action each later turn

Example: A knight in plate (AC 18) shrugs off your friends' blows. Heat Metal (action) on his armor: 2d8 fire. He cannot take it off, so he has disadvantage on his attacks. Next turn, you burn him again (bonus action) and Shocking Grasp him with advantage (action). Do not cast Faerie Fire at the same time: you can keep only one concentration spell.

11. Grabbed or boxed in

When: Enemies hold you in a corner, a creature grapples you, or you must reach a ledge, a roof or the far side of a gap or locked door.

  1. Bonus action: cast Misty Step. You vanish and appear up to 30 ft away, in a space you can see. You only need to speak: no hands. Fey Touched lets you cast it free once per long rest. After that it costs a level-2 slot. T p.79
  2. Grappled? The jump takes you out of the grabber's reach, so the grapple ends. PHB p.290
  3. Action: you still have it. Fire Bolt a far target (120 ft), or Shocking Grasp from a better spot, or build the cannon again if it is gone.
  4. Not the cannon: Misty Step is your bonus action, so the cannon does not fire this turn. Keep your reaction for Shield.
free 1× per long restthen 1 level-2 slotbonus action30 ft jump

Example: Two bandits corner you in an alley and one grabs your arm. You say the word: Misty Step (bonus action) puts you on a crate 30 ft away, free of the grab. Fire Bolt (action) hits the archer on the roof. Shield stays ready for your reaction.

12. Dark place

When: A cave, a ship's hold at night, or a crypt. Or you want to see (or hide) with care.

  1. Darkvision 60 ft: you see gray shapes in the dark, but not colors. Friends without darkvision need light. PHB p.41
  2. Free (draw it): the Moon-touched dagger. In darkness the drawn blade sheds moonlight: bright light 15 ft, dim light 15 ft more. Your friends can see by it. Sheathe it again to hide. It also shows where you are.
  3. Small light: Magical Tinkering can make a coin glow, but only dim light 5 ft. T p.11
  4. Something hides in the dark: Faerie Fire (DC 15 Dex, 1 slot, concentration) outlines creatures in the cube: they cannot hide, and attacks against them have advantage. PHB p.239
  5. Scout: the homunculus has darkvision 60 ft and Stealth +5. Order it with your bonus action. T p.21-22
free: dagger, darkvision1 slot (Faerie Fire)concentration (Faerie Fire)homunculus

Example: A cave with no torches. You draw the dagger (free): a pale glow, 15 ft bright, 15 ft dim. The homunculus flies ahead and sees a bat swarm. Then you sheathe the blade so you do not wake them. Later a shape moves in the dark: Faerie Fire outlines it for your friends. The Cloak of Billowing swishes for style only: use your bonus action for it only when the cannon is quiet.

The swappable bits (Enhanced Defense, Intimidation) are in CONFIG at the end of this file. Check numbers against your sheet. If the DM changes a rule, the DM wins.
