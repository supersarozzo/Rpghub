2Level 4: Ability Score Improvement or feat

At level 4 you choose one thing: raise ability scores (+2 to one, or +1 to two) or take one feat. A feat replaces the score boost. Tasha’s p.13, PHB p.165. No score can go above 20.

❓ Question 91 · master chat
  • a) Fey Touched (a feat)
  • b) +1 INT and +1 CON
  • c) +2 INT

Master’s recommendation: a) Fey Touched. Why it is a good idea: INT 17 is an odd number, so +1 INT makes 18 and the modifier goes from +3 to +4. A second point (+2 INT = 19) would be wasted. Fey Touched gives that +1 INT and also two free spells.

Odd scores waste points. A score only gains a modifier at an even number. INT 17 → 18 helps right away. INT 18 → 19 helps nothing. A plan that reaches INT 20 by level 8: take +1 INT now (INT 18), then +2 INT at level 8 (INT 20). Any “+1 INT” feat fits this plan.

The score choices (numbers at level 5)

Why not Strength, Wisdom or Charisma?
  • +2 STR (12 → 14): +1 on Athletics and spear hits. Nothing else Skippy does.
  • +2 WIS (10 → 12): +1 on Perception and Wisdom saves. Small.
  • +2 CHA (8 → 10): removes the −1 on talking skills. Small.
  • “+1 INT and +1 something else” has the same INT result as card B. Constitution is the best “something else”, because CON 16 comes sooner (a later +1 reaches it).

3Prepared spells

Every artificer spell of level 1 and level 2 (Tasha’s p.12), plus Skippy’s Artillerist spells. You can prepare level 1 and level 2 spells now. The list changes after each long rest (it takes about 1 minute per spell level). Casting a spell does not remove it from the list.

How many can Skippy prepare?

INT modifier + half your artificer level, rounded down. At level 5 that is 5 with INT 17, or 6 with INT 18 or more. Shield, Thunderwave, Scorching Ray and Shatter are extra: they never count. Skippy has 4 prepared now, so he adds 1 spell (or 2 with INT 18). Tasha’s p.11.

Slots are few: 4 of level 1 and 2 of level 2. A good list has a heal, a defence, a party helper and a control spell.

4Swaps allowed on level-up

Tasha’s lets an artificer swap two things when a level is gained. Skippy gains two levels (4 and 5).

The rules

  • Cantrips (Tasha’s p.11): when you gain a level, you may replace one artificer cantrip you know with another artificer cantrip.
  • Infusions (Tasha’s p.12): when you gain a level, you may replace one infusion you know with a new one.
  • Prepared spells: not a level-up swap. You change them after any long rest (section 3).

⚠️ Two levels are gained, so a plain reading gives up to two cantrip swaps and up to two infusion swaps. The book does not say what happens when you gain levels together. Ask the DM to confirm.

Infusions: Skippy knows 4

Only 9 infusions can be learned at artificer level 5 (the others need level 6, 10 or 14). Skippy can have 2 items infused at the same time. Infusing an item takes a touch after a long rest. Infusions stay in the item until you swap them. Tasha’s p.12-13, 20-23.

Replicate Magic Item is a special infusion: it copies one named magic item. Every item you pick takes one of your 4 known infusions. The full list is in section 4b.

Cantrips: Skippy knows 2

Cantrips cost no slot. At level 5 their damage dice double. Skippy keeps 2 cantrips until level 10.

✅ Cantrip list: Tasha’s p.12. not your book = Xanathar’s cantrip.

4bReplicate Magic Item

One of the infusions in section 4. It lets Skippy make a copy of a named magic item. Each time you learn it, you pick one item. Each item you pick is its own infusion (Tasha’s p.22). Tap an item to put it in My picks.

⚠️ Rule 1: infusions go only on NONMAGICAL objects. You touch a normal object and turn it into the magic item (Tasha’s p.12). So the Cloak of Billowing is already magic: it cannot be infused or changed. A replica is a new item made from something plain, such as a normal bag or a pair of goggles. The Dungeon Master’s Guide entry of each item says what kind of object it needs.
⚠️ Rule 2: only 2 items at once. At level 5 Skippy can keep 2 infused items at the same time (the “Infused Items” column, Tasha’s p.10). A replica is one of those 2. If Skippy keeps a Bag of holding, only one other infusion can be active (for example Enhanced Defense). You choose what to infuse after each long rest. Each replica also uses 1 of the 4 infusions Skippy knows.
Attunement: an artificer can attune to 3 magic items at level 5 (4 at level 10). Items marked “needs attunement” use one of the 3 places. Your Moon-touched weapon needs none. The normal limit is 3 (Dungeon Master’s Guide). Tasha’s p.13 gives 4 at level 10.
Darkvision: Skippy is a half-orc and already has darkvision 60 ft. Goggles of night add 60 ft to it, so he sees 120 ft in the dark.

5Arcane Firearm

New at level 5 (Tasha’s p.18). It is a wand, staff or rod that makes your damage spells hit harder.

How it works

  • Make it: when you finish a long rest, use woodcarver’s tools (Skippy is trained in them) to carve sigils into a wand, staff or rod. That item is now the firearm.
  • Only one at a time. If you carve sigils into a different item, the old sigils disappear. Otherwise they last.
  • Use it: it counts as your spellcasting focus. When you cast an artificer spell through it, roll a d8. Add the number to one damage roll of that spell. Scorching Ray has three rays, so you add it to just one ray.
  • Cure Wounds and other spells with no damage get nothing from it.
Tip: the Enhanced Arcane Focus infusion (section 4) also goes on a rod, staff or wand. Put it on the firearm: you get +1 on spell attacks and ignore half cover. It needs attunement.

✅ Tasha’s p.18, PHB p.151 (arcane focus prices). A plain quarterstaff (2 sp) as the “staff”: ask the DM.

6The new loot

❓ Question 90 · master chat
  • a) dagger · b) rapier · c) scimitar

Master’s recommendation: a) dagger. Why: artificers are trained in simple weapons only (Tasha’s p.10). A dagger is simple. A rapier and a scimitar are martial, so Skippy would not add his proficiency bonus when he attacks with them.

Moon-touched weapon

A magic weapon that glows in the dark. All three choices are finesse weapons, so Skippy uses his DEX (+2). The numbers below are for level 5 (proficiency +3). The weapon does not add to hit or damage by itself.

What “proficiency” means: if you are trained with a weapon, you add your proficiency bonus (+3) to the attack roll. If you are not, you add nothing. Skippy’s artificer training covers simple weapons only (Tasha’s p.10). Half-orcs get no weapon training (PHB p.41). Two ways to fix it later: the Weapon Master feat (four weapons of your choice) or a DM ruling. The Enhanced Weapon infusion gives +1 on any simple or martial weapon, trained or not.

About the item

The Moon-touched Sword is a common magic item. In darkness the drawn blade gives bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light 15 feet further. The rules say “any sword”, so the DM added the dagger. not your book Xanathar’s p.138 (web list). Ask the DM if it needs attunement for your table (the standard version does not).

Cloak of Billowing

A cloak for style

A common wondrous item. As a bonus action you make the cloak billow dramatically, like a hero in a wind. It gives no bonus to any roll. Wear it for the look, and for roleplay. not your book Xanathar’s p.136 (web list).

1,500 gp: buy ideas

Skippy has 9 gp plus 1,500 gp = 1,509 gp. Prices are from the PHB (p.145, 150-154). Tap ideas to add them up. The DM decides what is for sale. You can keep the gold.

Renown

1 renown with the Eyes Arcana

Renown is a score for how much a group trusts you. You earn it by doing jobs for that group. More renown can bring a higher rank and favours. This is an optional rule in the Dungeon Master’s Guide (p.22, not your book).

At your table, the Faction Agent background gives 3 renown and Rank 2 (docs/TABLE-RULES.md). Skippy has 1 renown with the Eyes Arcana. What each rank gives is in the server’s Faction Document, which I have not seen. Ask the DM what 1 renown allows and how to earn more.

7HP: average or roll

Skippy gains two levels, so he adds hit points twice. Each time: a die roll (or the fixed average) plus his CON modifier. PHB p.15, Tasha’s p.10. If your CON modifier goes up later, your maximum goes up by 1 for every level you have (PHB p.15).

If you roll at the table

Roll 1d8 for level 4 and again for level 5. Table rule: you may reroll 1s. Type the numbers you keep (1 to 8).

Honest maths: the fixed average (5) is a little better than rolling with rerolled 1s (about 4.94 per die). Rolling only adds luck: a range of 30 to 44 HP, against a sure 38. Both are allowed. PHB p.15.
